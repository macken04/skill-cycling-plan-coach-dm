# Strength & Core - Week 2026-W40

Phase: Base, week 1 of the season (starts 2026-09-28). Row used: `new` / Base wk 1-3 (anatomical adaptation).
Intent: learn the patterns and let tendons and lower back adapt before any heavy work. Target: 2 × 8-12 at RIR 3-4, no failure sets, about 2-3 s lowering and a fast, controlled lift. **Wednesday is a load-finding session:** the weights below are starting guesses, so add load between sets until a set lands at RIR 3-4, then note the weights for next week.

Your reported 50 kg squat/deadlift is a rough anchor, not a max. For movements that are new to you (leg press, trap bar) the first guess is about 60% of it, then adjusted by feel.

## Wednesday - Full session (~50 min)

Placed the day after Tuesday's sweet spot and well before Sunday's group ride, so your legs are fresh for both.

### Warm-up (8-10 min)
- **Raise (3-5 min):** easy bike or brisk walk.
- **Activate and mobilise (~5 min):** glute bridge × 10, bodyweight squat × 10, hip-flexor lunge with reach × 5/side, thoracic rotation × 8/side, ankle rocks × 10/side, cat-camel × 8.
- **Ramp-up on the leg press** (working guess 60 kg): 30 kg × 5, 42 kg × 3, 50 kg × 1-2, then rest 2 min before the first working set. Keep every rep fast and easy.

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load / cue | Rest |
|----------|-------------|------------|------|
| Leg press (limited depth, knee ~90°) | 2 × 8-12 | Start ~60 kg, RIR 3-4; upright and controlled, stop at 90° knee bend: kind to tight quads and lower back | 2-3 min |
| Step-up (low box) | 2 × 8 / leg | Bodyweight, RIR 4; slow down, no push off the back foot: builds single-leg force for steep climbs | 90 s |
| Trap-bar deadlift (light) | 2 × 8-10 | Start ~30 kg total on the bar, RIR 4; flat back, push the floor away: hinge with less lumbar load than a straight bar | 2 min |
| Chest-supported row | 2 × 10-12 | Light dumbbells (~8-10 kg), RIR 3-4; squeeze shoulder blades: posture for long days in the drops | 90 s |
| Dead bug | 2 × 8 / side | Bodyweight, RIR 3-4; low back pressed into the floor: trunk stability for climbing out of the saddle | 45 s |

Do 1-2 lighter ramp-up sets before the step-up, trap-bar and row, not full sets.

### Cool-down / mobility (5-8 min)
5 min easy walk or spin, then hold each 30-45 s: half-kneeling hip flexor stretch (each side), thoracic extension over a bench or foam roller, supine hamstring stretch with strap, knee-to-wall ankle stretch, prone quad stretch. Quad stretching goes here, not before the lifts.

### Why this session
- **Leg press over back squat:** tight quads and lower back in `strengthInjuryNotes`. It gives the knee-and-hip extension that the cycling trials used, with an upright torso.
- **Step-up:** single-leg force is the closest match to the steep-climb power limiter you described, and it shows any left-right difference.
- **Trap-bar deadlift over straight bar:** the lower-back note. Start light with RIR 4: hinging stays submaximal until it is pain-free for two sessions in a row.
- **Chest-supported row:** Badlands is an ultra (long hours in position); chest support keeps the lower back out of it.
- **Dead bug:** anti-extension trunk control, chosen for the same lower-back reason; more trunk work is on Friday.

## Friday - Core only (~25 min)

An easy day on the bike, so this session adds no leg load. Warm-up: 3 min easy walk or spin plus glute bridge × 10, cat-camel × 8, thoracic rotation × 8/side.

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Load / cue | Rest |
|----------|-------------|------------|------|
| Side plank | 2 × 30 s / side | Bodyweight, RIR 3-4; hips high, steady: lateral trunk stability | 45 s |
| Pallof press | 2 × 10 / side | Light band or cable, RIR 4; resist the rotation, don't let the band pull you round | 45 s |
| Sorensen hold | 2 × 30 s | Bodyweight, stop before any pull in the lower back: back-extensor endurance for long rides | 60 s |
| Bird dog | 2 × 8 / side | Bodyweight, RIR 4; slow, no hip wobble | 45 s |

Cool-down: 5 min of the same holds as Wednesday, focusing on hip flexors and thoracic extension.

### Why this session
- **Side plank and Pallof press:** anti-rotation and lateral stability, the core work that supports steady power on climbs.
- **Sorensen hold and bird dog:** back-extensor endurance and control for the lower-back note. Stop either if you feel pain above 3/10.

## Progression note

Weeks 1-3 stay at 2 × 8-12 with RIR 3-4. Add load (+5-10 kg leg press or trap bar, +2.5-5 kg on the row) only once every set reaches the top of the rep range at RIR 3-4 on two sessions in a row. Weeks 4-8 move to 3 × 6-8 at RIR 2-3. If the lower back or quads flag discomfort above 3/10 lasting more than a day, or you are still very sore at 48 h (6/10 or worse), tell me and we will cut volume before adding any load. Next week's Wednesday uses the weights you find today.
